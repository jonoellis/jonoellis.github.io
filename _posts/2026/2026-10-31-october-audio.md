Celeb Traitors, Dutch visitors, beer bottling, school concert + refreshments volunteering, #cabin stay (Culloden/Nairn in the sun with the cousins, Redpoint in Yellow winds, 60+mph winds, drookings at Gairloch, chimney leak repairs), Moulin Vaddie (5 star views and great company in half star accommodation), games night, working extra to build flexi, Amber Weather Warning and subsequent destruction, games of Pig Hole (Schweinchenspiel) with pals, a few days at the parents' helping take down trees/fix shelving/tech support, all ending with another emergency #cabin trip for a leak.
