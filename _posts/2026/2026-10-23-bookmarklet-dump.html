www=stage

A little switcheroo that changes the www part of a URL to "stage" or vice versa... feel free to substitute "stage" with your own staging environment subdomain.

www=stage 

javascript:(function(){var%20loc=location.href;if(loc.indexOf(%22www%22)>=0){loc=loc.replace('www','stage');}else{loc=loc.replace('stage','www');}location.replace(loc)})()
