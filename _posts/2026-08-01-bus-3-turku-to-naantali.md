Luggage lockers out of order, someone missed a flight because of it (although not today, I hope!). Little cafe's temporary luggage storage service is coining it in. Bus stop location confusion overcome. Blazing sun. Tired feet. I crashed in to everyone getting onto the bus - sorry Turkuvians. Saturday afternoon suburbs then car showrooms, BBQ restaurants, tennis courts, construction, food factories, tool shops, graveyards, play parks, ring roads all pass by. We're sad not to be able to experience the 24h K-Market. Tiny air-con puffs to the face. Overhead usb ports for recharging. Funky pylons, two legged, boxy, support cables overhead between lots. Moi Moomins!
