Dumped bags in cabins and acted like locals by bagging a departure canteen table. Restaurant cart for the win - vegan pesto pasta with vege balls, vegan churros and vegan muffin all delicious. Six fingered hand outline design on carriage doors thew me. Much post-dinner bed chilling, blogging and watching view. K-MARKET resplendent in the sunset. Midnight light. Empty six lane highways. Radio towers, radio masts and pylons. If feels weird sleeping in daylight after a rainy day! Awakened to the train standing in a station and briefly thought since it was broad daylight that we were at our stop...  but no, 2h to go. Awake for the day at 5am! Smooth ride to Tampere and ready for a sunny day (at 7am!)
