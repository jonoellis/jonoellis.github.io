Broken down train caused chaos on the airport route. No final use of our rail passes but the alternate Flytoget airport express got us there. I feel this is emblematic of train trips - train timetables aren't dependable so leave padding. We're all facing backwards but at least have seats. On-train luggage tag printing services available to get the jump on airport queues (train too squished to attempt). Many vintage trains parked on the sidings. The "express" train goes at walking speed for a bit - a few passengers looking very tense. We pass a huge water spray fountain in the river. We arrive late but in plenty of time. There are rainbow arches over some of the security barriers for kids to go through: cute. Goodbye Oslo, you were cool.
